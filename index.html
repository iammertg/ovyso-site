Health widgets, crafted.

The steps, sleep, heart rate and vitals already in Apple Health, as Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets you design yourself.

For iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

Six layouts.

Metric, Dashboard, Rings, Trend, Lock Screen and Circle. They keep themselves current.

Design it yourself.

Pick a layout, choose which metrics it shows, choose a finish and a typography style, and preview it in every size before you add it.

Seven finishes. Aurora, Graphite and Paper are free; Coral, Ocean, Forest and Solar come with Ovyso Pro.

Walk with friends, in rounds.

A Race, Target Days, a Team Goal, a Team Streak, or a Journey. Invite anyone by link or a six-character code. Joining a circle is free.

Or walk the Camino together.

Your circle's steps move along a real route, and every stop reached sends an illustrated postcard. Six routes, or one of your own between two places.

Every round ends with a result.

A podium, awards, and laurels that lift your rank. The latest winner holds the circle's crown, and a season of contests crowns a champion. Rank, laurels and season finishes are earned, never sold.

Share it as a poster.

Your health data stays on your phone.

The exceptions are Circles and Friends, and only what they need: the day's steps and distance, a date, a time-zone offset and the name you chose — plus, in Circles, the goals you set, and in Friends, your username. Nothing else Ovyso reads from Apple Health leaves your iPhone.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking. The detail is in the privacy policy.

Coming soon to the App Store.

The core app is free. Ovyso Pro removes the design limit, unlocks all seven finishes and lets you host circles of your own — one payment, no subscription.

Ovyso reads Apple Health directly, so it works with an Apple Watch, Garmin, Oura, Withings, or your iPhone on its own — including the data your Fitbit syncs to Apple Health.
