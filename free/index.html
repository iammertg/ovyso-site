A free copy of Ovyso Pro

Pro is Ovyso's one-time unlock. This link hands it to you at no cost.

Redeem on your iPhone

Open this page on the iPhone you want Pro on. The link hands off to the App Store app, so a desktop browser shows an empty page and nothing happens.

How it goes

  1. Tap Redeem. The App Store opens with the offer and asks you to confirm. It costs nothing.
  2. Let it install Ovyso if you don't have the app yet. If the App Store doesn't offer to, install Ovyso first and tap Redeem again.
  3. Open Ovyso. Pro is on. If it isn't, Settings inside the app → Restore Purchases re-applies it.

What Pro is

Everything Ovyso keeps behind one purchase, which this covers in full:

  • As many widget designs as you like in the Studio, past the two the free tier keeps.
  • All seven finishes.
  • Circles of your own to host — joining a Circle somebody else invites you to is free, always.

It stays on your Apple Account for good. Ovyso has no subscription of any kind, so nothing is charged now or later.

The small print

  • One redemption per Apple Account.
  • There is a fixed number of them. When they run out, the link stops working.
  • The offer is Apple's own, redeemed through the App Store — Ovyso never sees anything about your account or your payment details.

If the link turns you away, write to support@ovyso.app and say what the App Store told you.
